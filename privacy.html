Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LevelUp Auto Body Inc, doing business as Level Up Auto Body ("Level Up Auto Body," "we," "us"), located at 21417 Ingomar St #4, Canoga Park, CA 91304, collects, uses, and protects information you give us through our website, estimate forms, phone calls, and text messages.

Information we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us, such as:

  • Your name and mobile phone number
  • Vehicle year, make, and model
  • Your insurance company and claim details, if you have a claim
  • A description and photos of your vehicle damage
  • Your text messaging consent choices, and the date and time you gave them

Like most websites, our site may also record basic technical data (browser type, pages visited, and the ad that brought you here) to measure how our advertising performs.

How we use your information

  • To prepare your repair estimate and contact you about it
  • To schedule drop-off, loaner cars, and towing
  • To send repair status updates and let you know when your vehicle is ready
  • To handle your insurance claim, when you ask us to
  • To send occasional promotional texts, only if you separately opted in to them
  • To answer your questions and provide customer service

Text messaging (SMS)

If you check the text message box on our estimate form, or text us first, you agree to receive text messages from Level Up Auto Body at the number you provided. Checking the box is optional and is not required to get an estimate or use our services.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages, or HELP for help. See our SMS Terms & Conditions for full details.

When we disclose information

We do not sell your personal information. We only disclose it:

  • To your insurance company, when you ask us to handle your claim
  • To service providers who help us run our business (for example, our phone, texting, and email providers), only as needed to serve you
  • When required by law

How we protect your information

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed to serve you, complete your repair, and meet legal record-keeping requirements.

Your California privacy rights

California residents may request to know what personal information we hold about them, ask us to correct or delete it, and opt out of any marketing. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will not treat you differently for making a request.

Children

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
