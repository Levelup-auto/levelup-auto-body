1. Program name and description

Level Up Auto Body Customer Text Messages. When you opt in, LevelUp Auto Body Inc, doing business as Level Up Auto Body, will send text messages to the mobile number you provided. These may include:

  • Replies to your estimate request and follow-up questions about your vehicle damage
  • Appointment, drop-off, towing, and loaner car reminders
  • Repair status updates and notice when your vehicle is ready for pickup
  • Replies when we miss your call
  • Occasional promotional messages, only if you separately opted in to them

2. How you opt in

You opt in by checking the optional text message box(es) on our estimate form, or by texting us first. Opting in is never required to get an estimate, request service, or do business with us.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your repair and your requests. Promotional messages, if you opted in, are limited to no more than 4 per month.

4. Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply. Check your mobile plan for details.

5. How to cancel (STOP)

You can cancel at any time. Simply text STOP to the number that messaged you. Upon sending STOP, we will confirm your unsubscribe status via SMS. After that, you will no longer receive text messages from us unless you opt in again. You can also reply UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT.
